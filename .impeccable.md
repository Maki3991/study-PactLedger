## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are hackathon judges, technical reviewers, and the team member operating the live demo. They need to understand within seconds that Agent Treasury is a reusable financial control plane rather than a pair of unrelated demos. Their core jobs are to inspect agent accounts and budgets, verify policy decisions, follow an action from intent to approval and execution, and open an auditable receipt. KaleidoX stock research and PoolMate group buying are proof cases that submit actions into the same base.

### Brand Personality
Trustworthy, exacting, operational. The interface should feel like a serious financial control room: calm under pressure, explicit about system state, and unwilling to blur the line between replay, mock, and live execution. Copy should be concise and evidence-led.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing dark, restrained, industrial dashboard language with warm green as the approval/system-health accent and amber/red reserved for review and rejection. Prefer dense but legible operational layouts, sharp separators, compact typography, and visible provenance over decorative cards or generic AI imagery. Preserve responsive behavior and reduced-motion support. Avoid neon cyberpunk styling, purple-blue AI gradients, excessive glow, glassmorphism, and marketing-page hero patterns.

### Design Principles
1. Show the base through shared state: every case action must appear in the same intent queue, policy trace, and audit ledger.
2. Truth before theatre: label PandaAI, replay, mock, testnet, and live modes explicitly; never imply a transaction is verifiable without a real receipt.
3. Evidence is the interface: surface data source, date range, strategy version, policy reason, intent ID, and transaction hash at the point of decision.
4. Progressive depth: the first screen explains system health and current flow; details reveal exact checks and provenance without overwhelming the demo.
5. Reuse the current visual system: improve hierarchy and data binding instead of rebuilding the application as a separate landing page.
